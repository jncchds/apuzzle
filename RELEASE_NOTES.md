# Release notes

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-13
- New game: Orbits. Give every star its own region: one connected piece with the star in its exact middle, looking the same after half a turn around it. Drag from a star over the cells to add, and each one brings its twin on the opposite side along; tap a cell to clear it. With a tutorial, strategy lessons and explain mode, and in the daily challenges from 2026-10-14

## 0.7.2 - 2026-10-13
- Slide across cells to mark them all in one go, in every tap-to-fill game (Tetra, Crowns, Islands, Lamps…). The slide repeats what the tap did to its first cell, skips cells that hold something else, and is one undo step
- Crowns, Campsite and Pairs: a long press puts a dot (grass) on every cell that the crowns, tents or pairs you have placed already rule out. A right click still cycles back
- Shikaku: a rectangle is drawn faint while it holds no number, several numbers, or a number that differs from its size
- Region outlines have rounded corners

## 0.7.1 - 2026-10-10
- Home screen: games are sorted into groups (Numbers, Logic grids, Paths & loops, Casual). Tap a group's title to fold it away; the app remembers which groups are folded
- New game: Formula. Guess the hidden equation in six tries: every guess is a true equation, and each tile is marked as in place, elsewhere or not there. Six, eight or ten tiles wide; easy boards add and subtract, medium ones multiply and divide too, hard ones have brackets, squares and cubes. With a tutorial and strategy lessons, and in the daily challenges from 2026-10-10
- Daily challenges: choose which games they draw from (the new button on the calendar screen; at least three). A change counts from today, and earlier days keep their games and results

## 0.7.0 - 2026-10-10
- Landscape: turn your phone (or widen the window) and the board fills the height, with the controls in a side panel; tutorials and the daily calendar too
- New game: Tally. Every cell holds a number: cross some out so the numbers left in each row and column add up to the sums outside the grid. With a tutorial, strategy lessons and explain mode, and in the daily challenges from 2026-10-10

## 0.6.1 - 2026-10-08
- New game: Snake. Swipe (or use the arrow keys) to steer the snake to the apples, grow longer and don't crash. Free play chases a best score, or pick an apple target; the difficulty sets the speed. Tap the board to pause. With a tutorial, and in the daily challenges from 2026-10-08 on a bigger board at each level
- Daily challenges: 2048 is now one puzzle a day (build 1024) instead of three

## 0.6.0 - 2026-10-02
- Explain mode for every logic puzzle (all but Mosaic, Blend, Pop, 2048, Pipes and Labyrinth): the new button next to Hint explains the next step in words, naming cells like "E4" and showing the board's own symbols, with the reasons behind it (and, for harder steps, the "suppose…" that leads to a contradiction). "Do it" plays the step, and you can switch back to playing any time. Entering the mode and each step count as hints
- Hint now plays the next logical step instead of a random cell

## 0.5.1 - 2026-10-02
- Settings: move your progress to another device as a file or as copied text; importing merges, so wins, best times and daily results from both devices are kept
- Solving a daily puzzle as a free game (for example from a shared link) now counts for that day too

## 0.5.0 - 2026-09-30
- Rails: tap a cell to mark it as track or put a "no track" dot on it; track notes count toward the row and column numbers
- Sudoku, Blocks and Plots: pencil marks stay under a placed value and come back when the value is removed
- Hues: pencil marks for candidate colors
- Release notes in Settings
- About & licenses shows the app version, copyright and credits
